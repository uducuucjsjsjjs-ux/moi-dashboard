Moi Dashboard

Moi Dashboard (in Russian: «Мой дашборд») is a personal organizer for Windows and Android. It helps one person keep track of their sleep, study schedule and deadlines, commute and game timers, and works offline with local data on the device.

What the Google sign-in is for

The app can optionally sync its own data between the user's PC and phone through Google Drive. For this it asks for a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which gives access only to a hidden application-data folder created by the app in the user's own Google Drive. The app cannot see or change any other files in Drive. The data is used only to show the same records on the user's own devices.

Moi Dashboard is a personal project and is used by its author and invited friends. It has no servers, no advertising and no analytics.
